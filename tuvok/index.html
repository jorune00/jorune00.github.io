Tuvok Assistant

Tuvok is a private, non-commercial AI assistant operated by Johnny Heintz.

What Tuvok does

Tuvok helps manage the dedicated tuvok.assistant@gmail.com account, monitors incoming mail, marks processed messages, and sends messages that Johnny has authorized, including a scheduled daily weather update. Tuvok can also work with files that the account creates or selects in Google Drive.

Google account access

Google access is used only after the account owner grants consent. The integration requests the minimum practical permissions needed to read and organize the assistant inbox, send authorized mail, and work with files created or selected through the integration. Tuvok is not offered to the public and does not use Google data for advertising.

Contact

Questions about Tuvok or its Google account access can be sent to tuvok.assistant@gmail.com.
