Tuvok Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy describes how Tuvok Assistant, a private AI assistant operated by Johnny Heintz, accesses and uses Google user data. The integration is intended only for the dedicated tuvok.assistant@gmail.com account and is not offered as a public service.

Google data accessed

After the account owner grants consent, Tuvok may access:

  • Gmail messages, message metadata, threads, labels, and mailbox profile information.
  • Permission to modify mailbox state, such as marking processed messages as read or applying labels.
  • Permission to send email from the authorized account.
  • Google Drive files created by the integration or explicitly selected for use with it.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to provide the account owner's requested assistant functions, including:

  • Monitoring and organizing the dedicated assistant inbox.
  • Summarizing or classifying messages and notifying Johnny when attention is needed.
  • Sending messages that Johnny has authorized, including scheduled weather reports.
  • Working with files created or selected through the Google Drive integration.

Processing and disclosure

Tuvok runs through a privately operated OpenClaw installation. Data may be processed by the configured AI service provider solely to perform the requested assistant function. Email is disclosed to its intended recipient when an authorized message is sent. Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and limited operational data are stored on a dedicated, access-controlled computer. Operational data may include message IDs, timestamps, sender or subject details, deduplication state, and task logs. Data is retained only as long as needed for the assistant's operation, troubleshooting, security, or the account owner's instructions.

Security

OAuth tokens are stored in a protected local credential file, access is limited to the assistant's operating account, and public web traffic to this policy uses HTTPS. No security measure is perfect, but reasonable safeguards are used to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

Your controls

The Google account owner can revoke Tuvok's access at any time from the Google Account connections page. The owner can also request deletion of locally retained Google-derived data by contacting the address below. Revoking access stops future Gmail and Drive access but does not recall email already delivered to its recipients.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tuvok Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or requests concerning this policy can be sent to tuvok.assistant@gmail.com.
